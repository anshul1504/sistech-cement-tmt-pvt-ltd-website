Cement range

Choose the Right Cement Category

Each cement category serves different project and performance considerations. Confirm the required product type with the approved project specification.

OPC

Ordinary Portland Cement

Conventional Portland cement for suitable concrete and structural applications where OPC is specified.

Selection focus: specified grade, early-strength requirement and approved concrete design.
PPC

Portland Pozzolana Cement

Fly-ash-based blended cement intended for projects where PPC is included in the approved specification.

Selection focus: workability, curing practice and project exposure conditions.
PSC

Portland Slag Cement

Slag-blended cement for suitable applications where PSC characteristics are required by design.

Selection focus: applicable specification, site conditions and engineering approval.

Buyer awareness

How to Identify Quality TMT Bars

Use visible checks together with applicable documentation and qualified project approval before accepting reinforcement steel.

01

Uniform Rib Pattern

Look for evenly formed and consistent ribs that support effective bonding with concrete.

02

Clean Surface Condition

Inspect for excessive rust, oil, scale, damage or other visible surface concerns before use.

03

Certification & Traceability

Verify applicable ISI/BIS marking, grade identification and supporting test documentation.

04

Consistent Diameter

Check that the supplied diameter and weight correspond with the approved material requirement.

05

Trusted Supply Source

Purchase through an authorised, traceable supplier and retain invoices and batch information.

Do not rely on appearance alone.Material acceptance should follow applicable standards, mill/test certificates and the project engineer's approved specification.
