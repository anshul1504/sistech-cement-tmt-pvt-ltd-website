Program purpose

Celebrating Engineering Contribution

Engineers help connect material selection, technical understanding and successful project execution. The Star Engineer Program recognises eligible professionals whose annual engagement and conduct contribute to better construction outcomes.

Professional knowledge creates greater value when it is supported by collaboration, consistency and trust.
