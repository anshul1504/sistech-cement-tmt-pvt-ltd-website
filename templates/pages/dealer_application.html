Common questions

Dealership FAQs

Quick answers before you submit your application.

Who can apply for a SISTECH dealership?+

Existing building-material retailers, wholesalers, distributors, contractors and serious new entrepreneurs may apply. Selection depends on business suitability and territory availability.

Is GST registration mandatory?+

You may submit the initial application without a GST number. Required registrations and documents will be confirmed during verification based on the proposed business structure.

How much investment is required?+

Investment varies by territory, product category and expected market scale. Select your indicative range in the form and our team will discuss the suitable model with you.

How long does the review take?+

The team first checks territory availability and the applicant’s business profile. Shortlisted applicants are contacted directly for the next-stage discussion and verification.

Does submitting the form guarantee a dealership?+

No. Submission records your interest. Appointment is subject to internal review, document verification, market potential, territory availability and company policy.
