Programme purpose

Recognising the People Behind Stronger Structures

The scheme celebrates the contribution of registered masons and contractors whose workmanship, performance and consistent professional practices support dependable construction outcomes.

Recognition is not only about results—it is about the skill, discipline and partnership behind every result.

Who Can Be Considered?

The brochure identifies four key eligibility considerations for the annual scheme.

ImportantFinal eligibility and selection remain subject to programme rules, performance verification and company approval.

    Registered Mason or Contractor

    Participation is intended for registered professionals associated with the applicable programme.

  2. 02
    Annual Performance

    Recognition considers performance across the relevant annual programme period.

  3. 03
    Consistent Use of SISTECH Products

    Consistent product association forms part of the programme consideration.

  4. 04
    Compliance With Company Policies

    Participants are expected to follow applicable programme and company policies.
